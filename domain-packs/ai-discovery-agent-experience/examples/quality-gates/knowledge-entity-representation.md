# Skill Quality Gate — knowledge-entity-representation

## Proposal

- **Proposal id:** `AI-DAE-01-knowledge-entity-representation`
- **Proposal type:** `new_skill`
- **Target skill(s):** `knowledge-entity-representation`
- **Reviewer:** `catalog reviewer`
- **Source refs:** supplied AI Discovery & Agent Experience proposal; MoradaHarmoniA pilot scaffold

## 1. Necessity

- Recurring problem: digital properties represent people, brands, methods, products, and claims
  inconsistently, degrading downstream search and generative interpretation.
- Available evidence is proposal-stage plus a named pilot; field recurrence is not yet proven.
- Without a bounded method, entity reconciliation is scattered across content, schema, SEO, and
  brand work with no shared evidence contract.

**Assessment:** `partial`

## 2. Distinctness

- `domain-language-alignment` reconciles vocabulary, not entity identity and claim provenance.
- Knowledge-governance skills evaluate or reconcile sources, not representations across surfaces.
- Search and generative skills consume stable entities; they should not own the representation canon.

**Assessment:** `strong`

## 3. Proportionality

- Five durable Core Moves.
- Seven trigger-scoped modules remain optional.
- Shared templates are optional and no runtime, crawler, graph database, or publication engine is added.

**Assessment:** `strong`

## 4. Context discipline

- Minimum context: target entities, surfaces, decision, owner, and available evidence.
- Private consumer content, credentials, unpublished claims, and project state stay outside canon.

**Assessment:** `strong`

## 5. Verification

- Output separates entities, relationships, claims, facts, inferences, hypotheses, conflicts, and
  unavailable evidence.
- Failure signals include invented identifiers, hidden disputes, or machine-first wording that
  distorts human meaning.

**Assessment:** `strong`

## 6. Governance boundary

- The skill recommends representation guidance but does not settle ownership, legal, brand, policy,
  or publication decisions.

**Assessment:** `strong`

## 7. Decision

`accept_as_new_skill`

## Decision rationale

- It owns a stable representational task that precedes several other pack capabilities.
- Adjacent generic skills do not produce its entity-and-claim contract.
- Experimental status and synthetic-first validation preserve the incomplete field-evidence boundary.
