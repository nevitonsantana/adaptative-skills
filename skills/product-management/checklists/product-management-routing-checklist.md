# Product Management routing checklist

Use for substantial product framing, discovery, prioritization, metrics, or composition work. Do not apply it to a small wording correction.

- [ ] Is the product decision explicit?
- [ ] Is the desired outcome separated from the proposed output?
- [ ] Is the affected user, customer, or operator known?
- [ ] Are facts, hypotheses, assumptions, and requests separated?
- [ ] Is the decision stage classified?
- [ ] Is the smallest sufficient module selected?
- [ ] Were unsuitable methods named when method choice mattered?
- [ ] Are missing evidence and uncertainty visible?
- [ ] Are existing skills used through handoff instead of duplicated?
- [ ] Are human-owned decisions explicit?
- [ ] Is the output verifiable by a stated next step or stop condition?
- [ ] Are external references attributed without copied text?
- [ ] Are public documentation and canonical contract changes aligned?
