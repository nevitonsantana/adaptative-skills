# Observation

## Summary

Synthetic composition and conflict tests preserved investment and roadmap decisions for a human
owner. They kept stakeholder pressure, value evidence, complexity cost, and technical planning
as distinct questions.

## Why this attribution is plausible

This behavior is stated in the Product Management handoffs and in Feature Planning's
feature-worthiness boundary. The review required no new module or authority.

## Why this result mode is valid

`reinforced` is appropriate because the tests supported existing sequencing and decision
boundaries without proving a real investment outcome.

## Follow-up

Observe repeated real product requests before considering a refinement to module selection.
